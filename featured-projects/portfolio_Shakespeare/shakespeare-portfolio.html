The Problem

YouTube is a black hole for your time

You watch a 45-minute tutorial. A week later you need one command, and you're scrubbing again. Video is hard to search, skim, or Ctrl+F.

03
Notes go stale
Manual notes miss context. You never capture everything the first time.
04
Transcripts aren't readable
Auto-captions are a wall of text with no structure.
The Solution

Paste a URL. Get a clean transcript. Move on.

Shakespeare extracts the full transcript, formats it into natural paragraphs using a silence-gap heuristic, saves it as a .txt file named after the video, and auto-deletes old files after 7 days so your folder stays clean.
